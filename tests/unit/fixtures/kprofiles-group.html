Domundi TV Profile: History, Artists and Facts

Domundi TV (ดูมันดิ TV) is a Thai production company and talent agency. The company originally started out as a content creator group and was created by Aoftion Kittipat Jampa, along with actors Mark Sorntast, Poppy Ratchapong and Max Kornthas. The agency is a subsidiary of Mandee Work and was founded on August 3, 2016.

Official Company Name: Domundi TV (ดูมันดิ TV)
