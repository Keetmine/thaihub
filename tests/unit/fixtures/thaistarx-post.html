FORCEBOOK FUNTOPIA FANCON 2026

Presented by popular GMMTV duo GMMTV artists Force Jiratchapong Srisang and Book Kasidet Plookphol, the exclusive fan celebration FORCE BOOK FUNTOPIA FANCON is set to make a grand return in 2026!

Inspired by the theme “FUNTOPIA,” this event transforms the venue into a dreamlike city filled with joy and unforgettable memories. Through live musical performances, specially designed stage productions, and close interactive segments, Force and Book will invite fans into a world created just for them — showcasing a side of their charm beyond what audiences see on screen.

📅 Event Information

Date: Saturday, April 4, 2026

Venue: Union Hall, Union Mall, Bangkok, Thailand

🌐 The event will also be available worldwide via live streaming on TTM LIVE.
